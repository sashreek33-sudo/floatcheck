Victorian Public Holidays and Your P6 Schedule: What Most Planners Get Wrong

2026 & 2027 Victorian public holiday calendar · working days calculator below

Victoria observes the standard national public holidays plus three of its own: Labour Day, the Friday before the AFL Grand Final, and Melbourne Cup Day (metro Melbourne only). Victoria also treats Easter Saturday as a public holiday, which most other states don't. Get any of these wrong in a P6 calendar and every downstream date — early finish, late finish, float — is quietly wrong too.

Why this trips planners up

Two things go wrong most often. First, planners import a generic "Australia" calendar into P6 that only carries national holidays — missing Labour Day, Easter Saturday, AFL Grand Final Friday and Melbourne Cup Day entirely. Second, the AFL Grand Final Friday date isn't fixed years in advance the way Christmas is — it depends on the AFL's finals fixture for that season, so a calendar built in one year for a schedule running two or three years out needs updating once the date is confirmed, not left on last year's date.

What this means for planners

Why AFL Grand Final Friday catches people out every year

Unlike every other date on the Victorian calendar, Grand Final Friday isn't known with certainty until the AFL publishes its finals series draw, typically only a few months before the day itself. A P6 calendar built well in advance of a project's actual delivery window can easily be scheduled with last year's date, or with no allowance for the holiday at all if it was built before the date was public.

How to model it in P6 as a calendar exception

Treat it the same way as any other single-day exception: add it as a non-working day to every calendar that governs site or delivery activities, and flag it in your document control process as a date to revisit and reconfirm each year once the AFL fixture is released — not a one-time calendar setup you can forget about.
