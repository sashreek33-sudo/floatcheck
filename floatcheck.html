Greedy least-cost heuristic: crashes the cheapest currently-critical activity one day at a time and re-derives the critical path each step. Correct for a single dominant critical path; where parallel near-critical paths exist, a full LP-based multi-path optimisation may find a lower-cost combination — treat this as a strong first-pass estimate, not a contractual figure.
