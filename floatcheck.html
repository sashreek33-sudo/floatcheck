Example output — generated from this exact demo project

Live generation needs a backend to hold the API key server-side, which isn't connected in this build yet (see disclaimer above). The narrative below is a real, one-off output this feature produced from this project's own demo data, shown here so the feature isn't just a dead button.

Executive Summary

The project finish date has moved from 05 Feb 2027 to 03 Mar 2027, a slip of 18 working days. Three delay events on the critical path drove this: a below-ground obstruction during secant piling, higher-than-designed groundwater inflow during excavation, and a supplier logistics delay to TBM assembly.

Key Variances & Root Causes

  • Secant Pile Wall – South (A2010): 6-day slip from an unforeseen underground obstruction (a redundant sewer pipe not shown on the services plan); logged as potentially EOT-eligible since the services survey was principal-supplied.
  • Bulk Excavation Stage 2 (A2050): 4-day slip from groundwater inflow exceeding design assumptions.
  • TBM Delivery & Assembly (A3020): 8-day slip from a customs hold on the cutterhead bearing shipment, not a manufacturing fault.

Critical Path Impact

All three delays sit on the critical path; the finish date moved the full 18 working days as a direct result. No previously non-critical activities became newly critical this update.

Recommended Actions

  • Notify the Principal of the A2010 obstruction under the relevant EOT clause, given the services survey was principal-supplied.
  • Confirm additional wellpoint capacity is sufficient before Excavation Stage 3 begins.
  • Follow up with the TBM supplier on the customs resolution timeline.
